Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-14. Provided in English only.

Karabakh Atlas is a map and community forum for exploring the Karabakh region. This page explains what information the site collects, why, and how to reach us about it. It works together with our Terms & Conditions.

What we collect

  • Account information: the name and email address you register with, and your password. Passwords are hashed with a salted, slow hashing algorithm before storage — we never store or see your actual password.
  • Forum posts: the text you submit, when you posted it, and (if applicable) which city or point of interest it's about.
  • A session cookie that keeps you signed in. It's functional only — not used for tracking or advertising.

We don't collect payment information (the site doesn't process payments), and we don't run advertising or analytics trackers.

Third-party services

Some features call outside services to work:

  • Weather (Open-Meteo): receives only the latitude/longitude of the place currently shown — no account or personal data.
  • Map tiles and imagery (CARTO, OpenStreetMap, Esri): receive standard map-tile requests (like any web request, this includes your IP address) — no account data.
  • Email delivery: when you register, a verification email is sent to the address you provided via a standard SMTP email service.

Forum moderation

Every post is held for review before it's shown publicly. Administrators can read pending submissions as part of that review. Once approved, a post shows your display name. You can delete your own post at any time; an administrator can remove any post.

How long we keep data

Your account and posts are kept until you ask us to delete them. There's currently no self-service "delete my account" option — email us at the address below to request deletion or correction of your information. Deleting a place from the map also deletes any forum posts about that place.

Children's privacy

Karabakh Atlas is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll remove it.

Security

Passwords are hashed, not stored in plain text. Login and forum posting are rate-limited to reduce abuse. Traffic to the site is encrypted (HTTPS).

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continuing to use the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.
